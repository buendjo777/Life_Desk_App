**Install:** open Terminal, paste this line and press Return.

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/buendjo777/Life_Desk_App/main/install.sh | bash
```

Desk opens with a Welcome screen: start your own desk, open a folder you already have, or look around the demo.
Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Works on Apple silicon and Intel.

If you download `Desk.zip` by hand instead, macOS blocks the first launch because the app is not notarised:
open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. The line above avoids that step.
