# Programme comparison (demo)

Dates live in [[Masters/Deadlines 2027|the deadline table]].

## Section 1 — Europe

### Alpine Institute of Technology — MSc Biomedical Engineering
- Structure: 90 ECTS over three semesters, thesis in the last one.
- Specialisations: bioelectronics, medical imaging, biomechanics.
- Labs: cardiac signal processing, wearable sensors; see [[Research/AF detection#Next|the AF project]].
- Entry bar: strong maths and signals background; two references.
- Cost: about 1,500 a year in fees.

### Lakeside Polytechnic — MSc Life Sciences Engineering
- Structure: 120 ECTS, a research project plus a six-month thesis.
- Specialisations: neuroengineering, biomedical data science.
- Labs: embedded systems for health, computational imaging.
- Entry bar: competitive; three references.
- Cost: about 1,600 a year in fees.

### Thames College London — MSc Medical AI
- Structure: one year, taught modules and a summer project.
- Specialisations: clinical machine learning, imaging AI.
- Labs: hospital partnerships for data access.
- Entry bar: 2:1 equivalent; two references.
- Cost: high; check the fee table.

## Section 2 — United States

### Harbor University — MSE Biomedical Engineering
- Structure: three or four semesters, thesis or project track.
- Specialisations: imaging, instrumentation, computational medicine.
- Labs: many; contact faculty before applying.
- Entry bar: statement of purpose matters most; three letters.
- Cost: high; partial funding is possible.

### Northbridge Tech — MS Biomedical Engineering
- Structure: two years, coursework plus a project.
- Specialisations: medical devices, AI in engineering.
- Labs: device design, signal analysis.
- Entry bar: letters must arrive before the deadline.
- Cost: high.
